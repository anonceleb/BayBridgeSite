Services

Freight and customs coordination, arranged per shipment.

Each service below is coordinated through partner carriers, NVOCCs, or licensed brokers as the shipment requires. Baybridge does not hold a customs broker licence and does not clear cargo directly — clearance work runs through licensed brokers, with us handling documentation and liaison.

Ocean freight coordination

FCL and LCL bookings arranged through partner carriers and NVOCCs, covering routing, scheduling, and shipping instructions.

Air freight coordination

Coordination for time-sensitive consignments, working with partner airlines and consolidators on routing and booking.

Customs clearance liaison

Coordination with licensed customs brokers, documentation preparation, and filing support for import and export consignments.

Via licensed brokers

Import and export documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading / airway bill, and bill of entry support, checked against the shipment before filing.

Origin and FTA compliance support

Proof of Origin, CAROTAR Form I, and preference claim support under India's trade agreements.

Project and oversize cargo coordination

Coordination support for non-standard and oversize consignments, scoped and arranged case by case.

Describe the shipment and we'll tell you what's needed.
