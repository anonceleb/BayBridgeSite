FAQ

Common questions

What does a commission-based agent do?

We coordinate freight bookings, documentation, and customs liaison on your behalf, working with partner carriers, NVOCCs, and licensed brokers, and are compensated on commission rather than a bundled freight rate.

[FILL IN — specific ports and airports regularly worked]. Coordination is not limited to a single gateway.

Do you handle customs clearance directly, or through a licensed broker?

Through a licensed customs broker. We coordinate documentation and filing support and liaise with licensed brokers for clearance; we do not hold a customs broker licence ourselves.

What documents do I need for an import consignment?

Typically a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, and — depending on the goods — a bill of entry and any origin or licence documentation. We will confirm the exact set for your consignment.

How are your charges structured?

Commission-based, agreed per shipment. Charges are stated plainly before work begins, with no bundled markups.
